Safety & Ground Rules

Version 2.3 · Effective 1 Nov 2026 · Last updated 28 Oct 2026

1. Footwear & kit

  • Turf or moulded studs only; no metal studs or spikes
  • Shin guards recommended
  • No jewellery during play

2. On the pitch

  • No smoking, gum, glass, food or sugary drinks on the turf
  • Max players per booking as listed
  • Respect the time — leave promptly for the next team

3. Safety

  • First aid and AED at reception
  • Report injuries to staff immediately — we log every incident
  • Evacuation point: main car park

4. Behaviour

Abusive behaviour towards staff, referees or players leads to removal without refund and may result in a ban.
